Ghurab is a competition team — robotics, hackathons, and CTFs — building toward
autonomous systems, industrial robotics, and cybersecurity.

We are engineering students. The work on this site is competition work, and the
services we offer are the capabilities that came out of it. We move fast and break
things on purpose, keep only what survives, and document the failures alongside the
wins — because the failures are usually where the real engineering is.

Most recently, we became the only team from Bangladesh to reach the podium in the
WRO Future Engineers category, building a self-driving car at a fraction of the going
cost and taking a bronze medal on a converter sourced from a friend at 3 a.m. the
night before. The full story, and the engineering behind it, is
[on the blog](/blog/) and [in the project archive](/projects/).

## The team

**Shamman Rahin — Technical Lead.** Owns the technical direction of the team. Designed
and built every circuit and every mechanism on the WRO vehicle, from the parallelogram
steering linkage to the two stacked single-sided boards etched locally in Dhaka. When a
decision came down to the hardware, it was his.

**Syed Sholok — Systems & Tooling.** A generalist across the stack. Built the full
competition arena and the stress-testing rig that made the robot antifragile before it
ever saw a real track, wrote the tooling that calibrated the computer vision, and designed
and coded the system architecture on the Raspberry Pi side.

**Solaiman Kalam — Computer Vision.** Led the early computer-vision work and the initial
Raspberry Pi bring-up. Solaiman has since moved on from the team to pursue other goals; his
contribution to the vehicle that medalled is part of its record.

## Where this is going

Right now, Ghurab is a competition team with a real result behind it. The direction —
autonomous systems, industrial robotics, and offensive-minded security — is trajectory,
not a finished product line, and we're honest about which is which. If you want in before
this becomes a company rather than after, that's exactly the moment we're built for.
